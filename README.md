# Cybersecurity for Autonomous Vehicles

This repository provides selected research artefacts derived from our study:

**Cybersecurity for Autonomous Vehicles: Review of Attacks and Defense**  
*Computers & Security*, Volume 103, 2021, Article 102150.

The study reviewed 151 papers published between 2008 and 2019 and systematically organised research on attacks and defensive approaches for autonomous vehicles.

## Repository Contents

- `taxonomy/attack-taxonomy.md`  
  Classification of autonomous vehicle attack research.

- `taxonomy/defense-taxonomy.md`  
  Classification of defensive approaches for autonomous vehicles.

- `CITATION.cff`  
  Citation metadata for the associated publication.

## Research Scope

The study classified autonomous vehicle attack research into the following major areas:

- Automotive control systems
- Autonomous driving system components
- V2X communications
- Risk assessment and review

Defensive research was organised into:

- Security architectures
- Intrusion detection systems
- Artificial intelligence and big-data-based approaches

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is not to reproduce the published article, but to make selected research artefacts easier to reuse, extend, and apply in cybersecurity research and education.
